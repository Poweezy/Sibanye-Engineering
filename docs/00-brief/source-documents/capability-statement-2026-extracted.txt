Musa is a civil engineer, program & project manager, public private
partnership specialist with extensive experience in leadership, corpo-
rate strategy and managing complex infrastructure projects. He has
vast experience in implementing projects in the Health Sector,
Agriculture Sector, Mining Sector, Energy Sector, Water Infrastructure
Sector, Commercial and Industrial Sector and the Transportation
Sector.
He has led as a Director of Engineering at local government responsi-
ble for leading a diverse team, MTEF budgeting, implementing the IDP
(2025 -2030) for Manzini Municipality, implementing and managing the
city infrastructure assets.
Frans Pienaar, a Swazi citizen, is currently an Investor and Independent
Director with over 30 years’ experience in the construction and built
environment. He has served as Inyatsi Construction Group Chairman
from December 2012 - 	October 2016. Having attained a Bachelor of
Science in Civil Engineering from Stellenbosch University in 1987, he
obtained registration as a Professional Engineer with the Engineering
Council of South Africa in 1993 and a Full Member of the South African
Institution of Civil Engineers in 2003 and was elected as a Member of the
Institute of Directors in Southern Africa in July 2006. Frans also serves
on several boards as a Board Director- Chairman of the Board of
Challenge Ministries Swaziland; Chairman of Board - The Luke
Commission; Chairman- Bulembu Ministries and Board of Directors-
